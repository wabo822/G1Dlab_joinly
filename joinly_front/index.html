How Joinly works

  1. 1

    Find a plan you like

    Browse by interest, date or distance. Every activity shows who's hosting, who's already going and how many spots are left.

  2. 2

    Join and say hello

    Tap Join to take a spot. You land in the group chat right away, so you can ask questions and get to know people before you meet.

  3. 3

    Keep the people

    Afterwards, connect with the people you clicked with, see what they're joining next and plan the next one together.

Already doing something? Open it up.

Post your weekly run, your book club or a one-off museum trip. You choose the group size, who gets in and whether there's a cost to split.

Post an activity
  • Approve who joinsOpen it to everyone, or review each request and see people's profiles first.

  • Make it a regular thingRepeat it every week and the same faces keep coming back.
For local businesses

Run a café, club or venue? Get found by people looking for plans.

Joinly connects you with people who already want what you offer. Show off your place, post events and offers, and welcome whole groups through the door.

  • Showcase your venue with photos, opening hours and what makes it special.
  • Promote events and offers to people whose interests match.
  • Host activities that bring in groups, not just walk-ins.
